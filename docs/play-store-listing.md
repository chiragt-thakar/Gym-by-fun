# Play Store listing kit (v0.1 closed test → public launch)

Source of truth for store copy and Play Console answers. Facts come from
`docs/security.md`, `docs/launch-checklist.md`, `docs/stages-roadmap.md` §7–§8 and
`app/lib/game/stages.dart`. If the code changes, update this file before the next upload.

**Copy rules (apply to every string below, every language):**
- General fitness information only. Never claim calorie burn, weight loss, toning, fat
  loss, "get fit in N days", or any health or medical outcome. Never imply that the app
  measures heart rate, fatigue or health.
- Say "a game for general fitness, not medical advice" in every full description.
- Use only the approved benefit phrases from `docs/stages-roadmap.md` §8 (e.g. "works the
  big leg muscles", "breaks up long sitting", "counts toward weekly active minutes").
- Never promise worlds that are not shipped. Current builds play Worlds 1–4 (Forest
  Awakening, Desert Trials, Neon City, Mountain Expedition) plus the Garba Quest event;
  Worlds 5–9 show as "coming soon". Say "more worlds coming", not dates.
- No links, prices or nudges toward payment outside Google Play (AGENTS.md rule 11).
- Hindi and Gujarati text below is a **draft**: it must pass native-speaker review
  (launch checklist §1) before upload.
- No other game names, characters, music or trademarks in copy, keywords or video.

## 2. English listing

### Short description (≤ 80 characters)
Primary (76): `Your phone camera is the controller. Squat, lean and reach through 4 worlds.`
Alt (73): `A home movement game: your body runs the adventure. Video stays on phone.`

### Full description (≈ 2,700 characters, limit 4,000)

```
Turn your living room into an adventure. In MotionQuest your phone's front camera is the
controller: squat to duck under branches, lean to switch lanes, raise your arms to open
the gates. No band, no mat, no extra gadgets — just you, your phone and a little space.

A GAME FIRST
• Run through Forest Awakening, Desert Trials, Neon City and Mountain Expedition — with
  more worlds coming.
• Calm the guardian at the end of World 1 in a rhythm boss battle.
• Play the Garba Quest festival event on the taali beat.
• A fresh Daily Quest every day, with a warm-up and a cool-down built in.

MADE FOR YOUR BODY, YOUR HOME
• Short guided calibration measures moves against your own comfortable range.
• Low-impact by default: half squats count, one arm is enough, no jumping unless you
  turn it on.
• Assist mode and easier levels whenever you want them.
• If the camera loses you, the game slows down and tells you why. You never lose a
  heart or progress because of tracking.

COACH KAVI
Coach Kavi, your fox-orange running guide, gives friendly tips after each run: try a
slightly easier or harder level, sink a little deeper "only as far as comfortable",
or move into better light. Kavi works offline and speaks English, Hindi and Gujarati.

GYM CREW
Join your gym's Crew with a code from your trainer. See a friendly effort board and a
weekly recap. Sharing is your choice — your gym only sees your first name and activity
summary, never your body measurements. Members of partner gyms get Premium free.

YOUR VIDEO NEVER LEAVES YOUR PHONE
Pose tracking runs fully on your device. Camera frames are processed and thrown away
instantly — they are never stored, never recorded and never uploaded. Only your game
progress syncs to your account, and you can export or delete your data in Settings.

WHAT MOVING CAN DO
Stages mix squats, side-bends and overhead reaches: moves that work the big leg muscles
used for sitting and standing, move the sides of the trunk and reach through the
shoulders. A short session is an easy way to break up long sitting and build a regular
movement habit.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Free: Daily Quest, all of World 1 including its guardian battle, and the Garba Quest
event. Premium unlocks the later worlds, their boss battles and more events, billed
through Google Play.

IMPORTANT
MotionQuest is a game for general fitness, not medical advice, and is for adults 18+.
Stop if anything hurts and use the in-app pain report. If you have a health condition,
are pregnant, or are new to exercise, check with a doctor before you start. Clear a safe
space around you and keep the phone stable at floor or table height.

Available in English, हिन्दी and ગુજરાતી.
```
